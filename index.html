Flash Station

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on your computer. Safari and Firefox can't talk to USB devices, so nothing can be flashed from here. You can still look around and use the simulated board.

Ready

Pick the board on the jig to start.

  1. 1Board on the jig

    3V5V

    Set the UPDI Friend switch to 3V before the pogo pin touches the board. Never 5V on Pulse: its UPDI pad feeds the mic's 3.3 V rail.

  2. 2Programmer

    Plug the UPDI Friend into the computer, then choose its port. Chrome remembers it next time.

    Speed

  3. 3Check the board

    Board powered from its USB plug, pogo pin on the UPDI pad.

    • 4Engine and lighting

    • 5Drive details

    • Erases the chip, writes the engine with its lighting, sets the BOOTEND fuse, then writes the ID card. Everything is read back to check it.

      Terminal fallback

      If the browser flasher misbehaves, the same job runs from the Mac's Terminal with pymcuprog (install once: pip3 install pymcuprog).
